1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how KarakusApp (the “App”), developed by Rıza Erdi Karakuş (“we”, “us”), collects, uses and protects information when you use the App. It applies only to KarakusApp. Each of our apps has its own policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Content you submitPhotos, text or audio you choose to submit to use an app feature.
  • Support messagesYour email and message when you contact us.
  • Support messagesYour email address and message if you contact us.

Collected automatically

  • PurchasesSubscription status, product identifiers and an anonymous app user identifier. Payment card details are handled by Apple and never reach us.
  • Usage & diagnosticsApp interactions, device model, OS version, approximate region, performance data and crash logs.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, contacts or advertising identifier (IDFA).

3. Device Permissions

The App asks for the following permissions only when you use a feature that needs them. You can change them at any time in iOS Settings › KarakusApp.

  • Camera / Photos / Microphone / LocationRequested only when a feature needs it, and only with your permission. Each is optional.

4. AI Processing

If an app offers AI features, the content you submit is sent over an encrypted connection to an AI processing provider solely to produce the result you requested. It is not used for facial recognition, is not sold, and is not used by us to train AI models.

5. How We Use Information

  • To provide the App’s features you request
  • To understand how the App is used and fix crashes and bugs
  • To manage purchases and restore unlocked content
  • To answer your support requests
  • To comply with legal obligations and keep the App secure

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

7. Data Retention

  • Submitted contentDeleted from processing servers after the result is returned (at most 24 hours).
  • On-device dataKept on your device until you delete it or delete the app.
  • Support emailsKept as long as needed to resolve your request.

8. Your Choices & Rights

  • Revoke permissions at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Delete data stored in the App from within the App, or by deleting the App.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, KVKK or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data. Email karakusapp@gmail.com and we will respond within the period required by law.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact karakusapp@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data sent from the App is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We limit access to the minimum necessary and choose providers with strong security practices. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

11. International Transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States and the European Union. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App or the law changes. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version. Material changes will be highlighted in the App.

13. Contact

Questions or requests about KarakusApp’s privacy? Email karakusapp@gmail.com and mention “KarakusApp” in the subject.

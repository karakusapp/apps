1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Area Measure (the “App”), developed by Rıza Erdi Karakuş (“we”, “us”), collects, uses and protects information when you use the App on iPhone and iPad (iOS) or Android. It applies only to Area Measure. Each of our apps has its own policy.

The App does not require an account. Your measurements, notes and photos are stored on your device. We do not operate our own servers for your content, and we do not sell your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Information stored on your device

  • Measurements and projectsShapes, points, distances, areas, names, folders, prices, plans and notes you create. Stored on your device.
  • Photos and voice notesPhotos you take or pick and voice notes you record for a pin. Stored on your device.
  • Imported filesKML, KMZ, GPX, GeoJSON, CSV and shapefiles you open in the App are read on your device.
  • SettingsUnits, map style, appearance and similar preferences.

Information processed by the App and its service providers

  • LocationPrecise location while you use the map or measure by walking. It is processed on your device and is not sent to our servers. See section 4.
  • Map and place requestsTo show maps, search places and name a measurement after its address, the App sends map areas, search text or coordinates to the map provider (Apple on iOS, Google on Android).
  • PurchasesSubscription status, product identifiers, transaction information and an anonymous app user identifier. Payment card details are handled by Apple or Google and never reach us.
  • Usage and diagnosticsApp interactions, device model, operating system version, approximate region, performance data and crash logs.
  • Advertising data (free version only)Advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking), device and app information, IP address and approximate location, used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads. See section 6.
  • Support messagesYour email address and message if you contact us.

We do not collect your name, contacts, or exact location history on our servers.

3. Device Permissions

The App asks for these permissions only when you use a feature that needs them. Each one is optional, and you can change them at any time in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Area Measure; Android: Settings › Apps › Area Measure › Permissions).

  • LocationTo show your position on the map and to measure by walking around a boundary. You can still measure by placing points by hand without it.
  • CameraTo take photos for pins and to show pins and plans in augmented reality (AR).
  • PhotosTo attach photos you choose. The App only sees the photos you select.
  • MicrophoneTo record voice notes, only while you record.
  • NotificationsFor the ongoing walk-measuring notification and a reminder before a free trial ends.
  • Tracking (iOS)Apple asks whether the App may use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalised ads. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are still shown but are not personalised with the IDFA.

4. How Location Is Used

Location is used on your device to calculate distances, perimeters and areas, to centre the map on you and to place AR content. It is not sent to our servers and is not sold or used for advertising by us.

When you start measuring by walking, the App keeps recording the boundary while the screen is locked or the App is in the background, until you stop. On Android this happens through a visible notification; on iOS the system shows that location is in use. The App does not collect location in the background at any other time.

5. Maps, Search and Augmented Reality

  • iOS: maps, satellite imagery and place search are provided by Apple Maps (MapKit). AR uses Apple ARKit on your device.
  • Android: maps and satellite imagery are provided by the Google Maps SDK, and place search and address names by Google’s geocoding service. Google receives the map areas, search text or coordinates needed to answer, together with technical data such as your IP address, under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • AR on Android runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Camera images are processed on your device to place AR content.

6. Advertising

The free version of the App may show ads, for example one full-screen ad when the App opens (never while you are measuring). Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device’s advertising identifier and other device information to show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where else the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the App’s Settings.
  • iOS: the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking when Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt asks. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Area Measure Pro has no ads.

9. Backup and Sync

  • iOS: if you are signed in to iCloud, your projects can sync between your iPhone and iPad through your own private iCloud account. We cannot access it.
  • Android: if Android backup is on, your measurements, photos and settings are included in your device’s encrypted backup in your own Google account and return on a new phone. We cannot access it.

10. Data Retention

  • Projects, photos and notesOn your device (and in your own iCloud or Google backup) until you delete them or delete the App.
  • LocationUsed in real time. Location is kept only as part of the measurements you save.
  • Analytics and crash dataKept by Firebase for a limited period (crash reports about 90 days, analytics up to 14 months), then deleted.
  • Purchase recordsKept by RevenueCat, Apple and Google as needed to provide your subscription and meet legal obligations.
  • Support emailsKept as long as needed to resolve your request.

11. Your Choices and Rights

  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions (iOS) or Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions (Android).

Email karakusapp@gmail.com and we will respond within the period required by law. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

The legal bases we rely on are performance of our agreement with you (providing the App), your consent (permissions and personalised ads), and our legitimate interests (keeping the App working and secure).
